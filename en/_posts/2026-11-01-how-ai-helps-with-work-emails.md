Email take a lot of time and are a good test for AI: short texts, a clear goal, a tone you can check.

## Three uses that work

1. **Summarise** a long thread before replying.
2. **Draft a reply**: you write the points, the AI shapes them.
3. **Change the tone**: same email, more formal or more direct.

## What to always check

Names, dates and amounts must be re-read by hand. AI can get details wrong with great confidence.
