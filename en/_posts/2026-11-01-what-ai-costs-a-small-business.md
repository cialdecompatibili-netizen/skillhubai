The cost of AI is not just the subscription. There are four items to look at.

1. **Tools**: subscriptions or pay-per-use.
2. **Setup time**: testing, fixing, documenting.
3. **Review**: someone has to re-read the output.
4. **Training**: helping the team use the tool well.

Prices change often, so check official sites before deciding.
