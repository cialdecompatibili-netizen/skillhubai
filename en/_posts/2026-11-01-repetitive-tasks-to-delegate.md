Not everything should be automated. A good candidate repeats often, follows fairly clear rules and is cheap when it goes wrong.

## Typical examples

- Sorting incoming messages or requests.
- Drafting first versions of product descriptions.
- Extracting data from documents that always look the same.

## Do not delegate yet

Decisions with major legal or financial consequences stay with a person.
