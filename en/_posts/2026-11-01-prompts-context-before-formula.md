Many people look for the perfect prompt. It usually works better to give the AI what you would give a colleague: who you are, what you want and who it is for.

## A simple structure

- **Goal**: what should come out.
- **Context**: situation, audience, constraints.
- **Example**: a text you like.
- **Format**: length and structure.
