A comparison based only on what the two READMEs state, with no tests of our own. We will update this post once we have used them on a real project.

| | MotionSites Web Builder | /awwwards |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Cinematic landing pages | A full site from a one-line brief |
| Strength | Media shelf (video, 3D cut-outs, images) and more than 55 technique recipes | Phased process from concept to delivery, with quality control |
| Stack | Pages built with the recipes' techniques | Next.js or Astro, with GSAP, Lenis and OGL |
| Quality control | Screenshots and automatic fixing | Audit, desktop/mobile screenshots and a score per category |
| Cost | Free part; the full library is paid | MIT licence, but long sessions that use many tokens |
| Other | "Prompt" mode for Lovable, Bolt, v0 and more | /jury skill to review any site |

## When the first
If you need **one high-impact page** (launch, campaign, product page) and want ready-made video and 3D, MotionSites.

## When the second
If you start from scratch and want **a whole site with its own identity**, and a long session does not scare you, /awwwards. Its /jury is also useful just to review a site you already have.
